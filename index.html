MarketLens

Overview

MarketLens is a stock analysis and backtesting tool covering 5 tickers (AAPL, MSFT, NVDA, SPY, JPM) with 20+ years of daily price history.

  • Chart — price and volume history with adjustable timeframes
  • Indicators — RSI, MACD, and Bollinger Bands overlays
  • Backtest — simulate 4 trading strategies against full history
  • Watchlist — save tickers you're tracking

Start on the Chart tab to explore a ticker.
